Privacy Policy

How KeyScope handles information and protects your privacy.

1. Introduction

KeyScope is an independent keyword and content research application operated by THE OUTLINE STUDIOS.

KeyScope is designed to help creators and marketers discover keywords, explore topics, identify content opportunities, and plan Pinterest-related content using data and functionality made available through authorized services.

KeyScope is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially connected with Pinterest, Inc.

2. Information We May Collect

KeyScope is currently in development. Depending on the features you use, we may process information that you voluntarily provide and information made available through authorized third-party services.

This may include:

  • Information you voluntarily provide when using KeyScope.
  • Search terms, keywords, or topics entered into KeyScope.
  • Information made available through authorized Pinterest API access, where applicable.
  • Basic technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

3. Pinterest API

KeyScope may use the Pinterest API to provide permitted functionality related to keyword discovery, topic research, content planning, and other Pinterest-related research features.

If you choose to connect a Pinterest account, authorization is performed through Pinterest's official OAuth authorization process. KeyScope does not request or collect your Pinterest password, session cookies, or other Pinterest login credentials.

KeyScope requests only the permissions necessary for the functionality being provided. Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used only to provide the relevant functionality to the authorized user and in accordance with Pinterest's applicable developer requirements.

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by KeyScope may be used to:

  • Provide and operate KeyScope.
  • Process keyword and research requests.
  • Provide permitted Pinterest-related research functionality.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the service.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

Information obtained through the Pinterest API is not used to train, fine-tune, or improve artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Pinterest Data

KeyScope handles information obtained through the Pinterest API in accordance with Pinterest's applicable developer policies and API requirements.

KeyScope does not sell or share Pinterest API information with third parties for their own purposes.

KeyScope does not use Pinterest API access to scrape Pinterest, circumvent Pinterest restrictions, manipulate Pinterest engagement, or perform automated actions that are not expressly supported and authorized.

KeyScope does not use Pinterest API information for unauthorized competitor intelligence, benchmarking, or similar platform-wide analysis.

6. Data Retention

KeyScope retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its services, maintain security, respond to support requests, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is handled in accordance with Pinterest's applicable developer requirements, including any restrictions concerning storage and retention.

Where Pinterest requires information to be retrieved from its API rather than stored, KeyScope will follow those requirements.

7. Account Access and Revocation

If you authorize KeyScope to access your Pinterest account, you may revoke that authorization through the controls provided by Pinterest or by contacting us regarding your KeyScope connection.

When access is revoked, KeyScope will no longer make API requests on your behalf using the revoked authorization, subject to any actions necessary to comply with legal or security requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

KeyScope may rely on third-party services for hosting, infrastructure, authentication, or API functionality where necessary to operate the service.

These services may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by KeyScope against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as KeyScope develops and its features change. When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
